Meet the founder

Made slowly, worn for years.

ODETTA is the family label from Abi Odedeyi. The name is carved from Odedeyi, and the idea is simple: fewer, better things for every member of the family.

Every garment is cut from natural fabrics and made in small batches by makers we know by name. The jewellery line pairs timeless classics with a quiet nod to Abi's heritage: Ayo, the pendant, simply means joy.

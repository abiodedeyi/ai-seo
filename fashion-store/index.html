Our Story

Made slowly, worn for years.

ODETTA is a family label built on a simple idea: fewer, better things for every member of the family. Timeless over trend-led, natural over synthetic, and made well enough to hand down.

Every garment is cut from natural fabrics and made in small batches by makers we know by name. The jewellery line is strung by hand: natural clay, wood and glass beads on knotted cord, made to be worn every day.
